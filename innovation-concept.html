Three-Tier Learning, Library & Venture Ecosystem · Chamba, HP

More than classes. An engine for Chamba.

Synapse is built as a three-story community institution behind the Post Office in Chamba. We combine small-group academic preparation on the Ground Floor, a silent study library and teaching fellowship on the First Floor, and a local venture, media and heritage incubator on the Second Floor.

The Three-Tier Architecture

Traditional coaching centres stop at exam memorisation. Synapse is designed so that academic rigour, distraction-free self-study, and real-world venture building happen under one roof.

Ground Floor · Tier 01

The Learning Commons

High-engagement micro-classrooms & applied skill studios

Strong fundamentals, Socratic questioning & practical fluency

The ground floor runs structured small-group sessions from 3:30 PM to 6:30 PM (Monday to Saturday). Instead of passive one-way lectures, classes combine board teaching, immediate problem-solving, daily exam practice tests, and dedicated doubt clinics.

  • Academic & Entrance Tracks (₹2,000/mo): Engineering & Research (PCM — JEE, IISER, NEST), Medical & Allied Health (PCB — NEET, AIIMS), CUET UG Domain/General, and UPSC NDA.
  • Communication, Business & AI Literacy (₹1,000/mo): Spoken and written English, business fundamentals, computational thinking, and responsible use of modern AI tools.
  • No-Device Testing & Real Work: Regular screen-free assessments paired with tangible student projects and presentations.
Small-Group Micro-Tuitions Daily Practice & Doubt Clinic AI & Communication Studio
First Floor · Tier 02

Silent Library & Fellowship Hub

Calm, distraction-free deep work space & earn-while-you-learn mentorship

A sanctuary for serious aspirants and peer mentors

Many students in Chamba have the talent to clear national exams or build careers, but lack a quiet, high-trust environment outside home. The First Floor provides dedicated study desks, curated reference books, and high-speed research terminals under a strict calm-computing norm.

  • Silent Study Library: Dedicated reading room for board students, droppers, college learners, and competitive exam aspirants (UPSC, state services, banking, UGC NET).
  • The Synapse Teaching Fellowship: Senior aspirants and sharp graduates can serve as Junior Fellows—helping run ground-floor doubt clinics and test reviews for a monthly stipend, free library membership, and career safety nets.
  • Curated Resource Bank: Standard NCERT/reference texts, previous-year test banks, and offline-first study workflows without social media distractions.
Distraction-Free Study Desks Teaching Fellowship Stipends Reference & Test Archive
Second Floor · Tier 03

Venture Studio & Media Engine

Incubating Chamba heritage brands, digital creators & local enterprises

Where Chamba’s cultural heritage meets modern D2C & software execution

The top floor is an active venture and prototyping workspace. Rather than waiting for outside jobs, students and local artisans collaborate to launch real commercial products, digital media channels, and software workflows from Chamba for national and global markets.

  • Heritage-to-D2C Brand Incubation: Modern packaging, storytelling, e-commerce, and supply-chain support for authentic Chamba Rumal embroidery, Chamba Chukh & specialty mountain pantry foods, and Gaddi Wool apparel.
  • Creator & Media Production Studio: Audio/video recording setup, product photography lighting, and design workstations for documentary storytelling and brand catalogues.
  • Software, AI & MSME Tooling: Building practical digital workflows—from automated GST/invoice validation and business marketplaces to calm, local-first learning tools.

The Self-Sustaining Community Flywheel

Synapse does not rely on external charity or high-fee coaching tropes. Every floor strengthens the others in a transparent, cash-flow-positive loop:

1. Accessible Tuition Affordable ground-floor courses (₹1,000–₹2,000/mo) and library memberships cover core building operations in Chamba.
2. Fellow Mentorship Top senior aspirants earn stipends as Teaching Fellows—guiding younger students in daily doubt clinics while preparing for their own exams.
3. Live Apprenticeships Communication & Business learners graduate from classroom exercises to real projects in the 2nd-floor Venture & Media Studio.
4. Local Wealth Creation Revenue and skills from D2C heritage brands and digital services flow back into student micro-grants, equipment, and local employment.
