03 / Governance Charter · Reciprocal Sovereignty

A federation, not a franchise.

Franchises extract brand royalties and mandate uniform mediocrity. A federation pools sovereign research strength while leaving the key to the front door entirely with the local district council.

Clause of Autonomy: No central headquarters may revoke a district's physical premises or dictate its curriculum beyond the universal axiomatic grounding.
