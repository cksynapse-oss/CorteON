01 / SCAFFOLDING

Hints before answers.

The machine refuses to surrender raw code or quick answers. It supplies micro-nudges that keep the cognitive burden on the learner.

02 / CALIBRATION

It asks how sure you are.

Knowledge without calibrated self-awareness is dangerous. Synapse measures whether you know what you know — and whether you know what you don’t.

03 / REALITY GROUNDING

The no-device quiz always has the final word.

Paper, fountain pen, whiteboards, chalkboard sessions. You can fool a prompt; you cannot fool a blank notebook in front of peers.

“A good tutor leaves its learner more independent, not dependent on next generation prompts.”

09 / PRINCIPLES
FOUNDATIONAL CHARTER

What we stand for.

01.

Think first.

The pencil and the clear sentence precede the code and the terminal prompt.

02.

Marks and a body of work.

We do not mock exam marks. We secure them, then build the real portfolio on top.

03.

Honesty.

No faked outcomes, no vanity certificates, no inflated hiring stats.

04.

Safety and dignity.

A quiet, respectful study environment for women, adolescents, and older citizens alike.

05.

Everyone teaches.

You do not truly understand a mathematical concept until you teach it to a 12-year-old.

06.

Local first.

District youth should not have to leave their family home to access elite mentorship.

07.

Open doors.

A retired civil engineer reading history has equal right to the building as an AI fellow.

08.

No hype.

We build with stone, wood, books, and disciplined code. Not temporary digital fads.
