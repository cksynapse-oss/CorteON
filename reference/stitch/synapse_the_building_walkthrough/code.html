05 / SEQUENTIAL ECONOMICS & INSTITUTIONAL DISCIPLINE

The floors are built in order.

A gurukul does not rely on venture debt. Each level earns the right to construct the next through verifiable local utility and earned cash flow.

MILESTONE GATE 01

Ground Floor First

It pays for the rest. Pod classes, computer literacy, and foundational tutoring generate immediate operating cash flow and community trust from day one.

Economic Condition Break-even at 80 local enrollees
MILESTONE GATE 02

The Library Follows Demand

The Knowledge Commons opens once 250 local scholars subscribe to the monthly quiet study card. Sustains silent reading carrels, book acquisitions, and fellow desks without municipal grants.

Economic Condition 250 Monthly Reading Cards committed
MILESTONE GATE 03

Venture Level When Builders Arrive

The top floor is unlocked only when local builders and startups commit to residency in exchange for mentoring younger scholars. No empty incubator real estate.

Economic Condition 4 Committed teams + 10 hrs/wk teaching
