04 / ARCHIVAL REPOSITORY —— DISTRICT KNOWLEDGE CODEX

The knowledge that escaped the textbooks.

Knowledge usually held by a few elders and then lost forever, collected, recorded and open-sourced: teaching material rooted in the specific geology and customs of the place, real problems for student projects and startups, and living pride for the district.

TOTAL ACCESSIONS: 1,420 CODICES
[ARCH-0142] TIMBER & JOINERY ARCHIVE
architecture

Oral Histories of Chamba Woodcarving & Temple Joinery

18 hours of studio audio recordings and 44 hand-drafted interlocking mortise-and-tenon diagrams dictated by master woodcarver Gian Chand. Captures earthquake-resilient kath-kuni structural joints unknown to standard engineering curricula.

FORMAT: MASTER AUDIO + CAD OPEN ACCESSION · AISLE 3B
[ARCH-0143] HYDROLOGICAL RECORDS
water

Upper Ravi Micro-Watershed Seasonal Runoff Log (1984–2024)

40 years of continuous daily gauge logs maintained by village forest guards across 6 micro-tributaries. Digitized, cleaned, and structured into open SQLite schema for local hydrological models and agricultural planning.

FORMAT: SQLITE + RAW LOGBOOKS OPEN ACCESSION · AISLE 1A
[ARCH-0144] VERNACULAR PHYSICS
thermostat

Traditional Himalayan Adobe & Slate Thermal Mass Metrics

In-situ empirical heat-loss measurements across 12 high-altitude dwellings over three winters. Proves superior interior passive heat retention compared to modern reinforced concrete block construction in sub-zero elevations.

FORMAT: SENSOR LOGS + MONOGRAPH OPEN ACCESSION · AISLE 2C
[ARCH-0145] APPLIED BOTANY
local_florist

Indigenous Wild Herbarium & Ethnobotanical Primer

160 verified pressed specimens of Pangi and Churah valley medicinal flora catalogued with phonetic Pahari nomenclature, seasonal harvest timing, soil pH indicators, and active compounds cross-indexed with pharmacological papers.
