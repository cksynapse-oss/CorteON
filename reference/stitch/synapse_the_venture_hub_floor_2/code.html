05 / ADMISSIONS & SELECTION PACT

We look for builders with patience and reciprocity.

Residency Evaluation Principles

CRITERION I

Proven Craft in Real Substrates

Demonstrated command over hardware, systems software, vernacular architecture, or applied biology. We evaluate existing code and physical prototypes, never slide decks.

CRITERION II

Pedagogical Generosity

A genuine, demonstrable appetite to dedicate 10 rigorous hours every single week to tutoring adolescents and fellows on Floor G in fundamental first-principles.

CRITERION III

District Constraint Grounding

A commitment to solve high-friction mountain constraints—intermittent connectivity, extreme gradient, thermal extremes—rather than ephemeral consumer apps.

CRITERION IV

Zero Vanity Speculation

Absence of growth-hacking tropes, inflated PR, or early fundraising obsession. The work speaks through physical reliability and district utility.

HONEST NOTE ON OCCUPANCY

The venture floor opens benches only when there are authentic builders of high caliber ready to fill them. We will run Level 2 at 25% capacity before compromising on the pedagogical covenant.
