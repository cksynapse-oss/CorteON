Where thinking gets lost

Modern AI tools are built for single turns. You ask, you get an answer, and the thread resets. Real problems don’t fit in one chat window.

Thinking doesn’t happen in one prompt

Chats, sketches and voice notes each hold a piece. None of them know about the others.

Context and reasons decay

The assumption three decisions rest on gets said once in passing and never checked.

Similar isn’t the same as useful

Finding a related note doesn’t tell you which uncertainty is blocking you.

The missing piece isn’t more information. It’s something that tracks where your understanding actually stands.

A system that works with how your thinking evolves

These are design hypotheses we’re working to test, not promises of solved intelligence.

Capture

Keep how a thought formed, not just the words

Thinking rarely starts as clean prose. Sketch while talking, or send a twenty-second voice note on a walk. Corteon keeps the timing and the strokes so you can replay the reasoning.

Preserve

Track how ideas rest on each other

Instead of flat folders, Corteon follows how an idea depends on an assumption, where evidence supports or contradicts it, and what is still unresolved. Every claim links back to its source.

Surface

Stay quiet until there’s something worth saying

No notification flood and no chat essays. At most three suggestions at a time: a missing bridge across time, a hidden bottleneck, or a concrete next question. You can inspect and dismiss each one.
